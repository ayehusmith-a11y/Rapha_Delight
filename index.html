About Rapha Delight

More than a food company

Rapha Delight Enterprise is an emerging organic food and agri-products company in Techiman, Ghana. We turn underused indigenous crops into nutritious, value-added products, while protecting the land and creating fairer opportunities for the farmers who grow them.

Our mission

To provide healthy, higher-quality products and services that improve the lives and total well-being of our consumers.

Our vision

To provide higher-quality food that creates economic value for everyone involved and makes a real social impact.

LocalBuilt around Ghanaian crops and producers.

The problem

Healthy food access still has gaps.

Poor nutrition, limited access to healthy food and unsustainable farming hurt both people and ecosystems. Demand for organic, gluten-free and functional foods is growing, but supply in many emerging markets has not kept up.

Our answer

Better products from responsible sourcing.

We build nutrient-rich products from responsible farming partnerships and cleaner production. The model supports healthier customers while creating economic and social value for local communities.
